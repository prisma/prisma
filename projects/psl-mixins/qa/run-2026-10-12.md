**Reproduction:** in `lsp/project`, open `shared.prisma`, add the line `  +Tenanted` after `deletedAt DateTime?` in `model mixin SoftDelete`, request `textDocument/definition` on `Tenanted`. Or: `cd $Q/lsp && node $D/qa-driver.mjs $C project $D/explore.json`.
