Beside GA

Asks

  • An ask has a new to do state: an agent proposes a plan, and a person accepts it.
  • An accepted plan files its own Linear tickets, and finished work moves on with no agent.
  • Search finds asks by meaning, and triage sees the ten closest asks.
  • The replies agent writes from approved templates and links the one docs page it read.
  • A contact’s page shows who they are, with a card per identity.
  • Agents outside Asks can register and receive signed events.
Asks is the system that tracks what people ask Prisma for on Discord and GitHub. It runs in parallel with GA, at lower priority.
